About Us

Khan's Logistics — Moving Business Forward

Who We Are

Khan's Logistics is a transport contractor and fleet owner based in Basavakalyan, Karnataka. We run full-load and part-load truck services across Karnataka, Maharashtra, Telangana, Gujarat and Tamil Nadu, carrying everything from construction material and FMCG to industrial goods and textiles.

Every booking is handled personally by our proprietor, Fayak Khan — from the first call to final delivery. There are no call centres and no middlemen, just a direct line to the person responsible for your consignment.
